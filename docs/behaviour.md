# How Pact behaves

This page lists the rules the app follows today, grouped by concept. It describes the code as it is. Where the code and a feature spec disagree, the code wins here and the difference is noted. The feature specs (`.scratch/pact-v0/spec.md`, `.scratch/vacation/spec.md`, `.scratch/anki-evidence/spec.md`) record the design as it was decided.

Terms are defined in [`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md). Paths are relative to `src/core/` unless they say otherwise. Every command and query goes through `AccountView` (`core.ts`), whose doc comments state many of these rules in full. A scheduler tick (`advanceTo`, `core.ts`) runs judging, reminders, bills, Oura and Anki checks.

Known bugs are marked **Known bug** and link to the ticket that fixes them. A ticket that changes a rule updates this page in the same change and removes the known-bug note it fixes.

## Pacts and schedules

- A pact has one evidence kind (Camera, Oura Ring or Anki), chosen at creation, and it never changes, not even in the edit window (`evidence-kind-fixed`, `editPact` in `pacts.ts`).
- Weekly count, Every 2 weeks, Once a month and One-time are for Camera only. Due-by is not offered for Oura Ring, and any HH:MM except 00:00 is a valid due-by (`checkedTerms`, `pact-rules.ts`; `isDueBy`). Test: `due-by-and-dates.test.ts`.
- The penalty must be more than zero. The recipient is just a name: repeats are allowed and a blank one is refused (`checkedTerms`). Test: `recipients.test.ts`.
- Oura Ring and Anki pacts have no description (`checkedTerms`).
- A pact has at most 5 reminders (`MAX_REMINDERS`, `src/shared/types.ts`). A reminder at or after the due time is refused (`reminder-after-due-time`, `remindersGoOffInTime`, `pact-rules.ts`). One-time reminders carry a date and time.
- A One-time pact takes no start or end date (`invalid-start-date`, `invalid-end-date`). An end date before the first occurrence is refused (`end-date-too-early`).
- The first occurrence is the first one due at least an hour after creation and not before the start date. A Weekly count pact's start date is a Monday (`start-not-monday`). It can be this week's Monday when this week is offered: when the days left for a photo, today included while its due time is ahead, reach the target, and the week ends at least an hour after creation; else it is refused (`week-too-short`). The target is the full week's. With no start date, a pact made on a Monday starts that week when it is offered, else the Monday after (`thisWeekStart`, `defaultFirstWeek`, `occurrences.ts`). The Schedule step offers this week (greyed out with the reason when not offered), next week (the default unless made on a Monday) and the next 12 Mondays, and under the end date the last whole week it gives (`weekStarts`, `pacts.ts`; `WeekStartField`, `pact-form-steps.tsx`; `.scratch/weekly-start/spec.md`). A start date stored before it had to be a Monday stays, and the pact starts the Monday after it. An edit in the window counts this week from creation; a change request checks only that a start date it changes is a Monday, and its form offers no this week. A One-time deadline less than an hour away is refused (`deadline-too-soon`) (`FIRST_OCCURRENCE_LEAD_MS`, `newOccurrenceTerms`, `occurrences.ts`). Test: `occurrences.test.ts`; `weekly-count.test.ts`, "the first occurrence" and "choosing the first week".
- The first occurrence is stored as a date. Its due time is worked out in the owner's timezone, so a timezone change before it opens moves it; once it has opened it keeps its timezone, as every occurrence does (`newOccurrenceTerms`; decided in pact-v0 ticket 02; see "Time and timezones").
- A pact can only be seen by its owner and its reviewers. Another person's pact answers `pact-not-found` (`ownPact`). Test: `pacts.test.ts`.

### Every 2 weeks and Once a month

- These are period schedules with one day to do: a period is two Mon–Sun weeks, counted from the pact's first, or a calendar month. One approved photo on any day of it Satisfies it; a period with none is Missed with one penalty. It's due at the end of its last day (`periodOccurrence`, `occurrences.ts`; `isOncePerPeriod`, `src/shared/schedules.ts`). Test: `period-schedules.test.ts`.
- The first period is the one that has the day the pact is made, unless it's due less than an hour later, or the first starting on or after the start date. The Schedule step offers "this" or "next": the next month, or the two weeks from next Monday. "This" is picked to start with when 7 or more days of it are left, counting today, and isn't offered when it's due within the hour (`offeredPeriods`, `firstPeriod`, `occurrences.ts`; `FirstPeriodChoice`, `src/client/pact-form-steps.tsx`). The client reads a period's kind from its length: 7 days a week, 14 two weeks, else a month (`periodKindOfSpan`).
- Reminders go off only in the period's **reminder window**, on a day with no evidence: its last 7 days. Before the window, Today shows the period in its own section after To do ("This month", "These 2 weeks", or "Due later" when mixed), as a small card with Take photo; from the window's first day it's a To do card with the days left (`reminderWindow`, `trips.ts`; `cameraToday`, `today.ts`; `worthReminding`, `reminders.ts`).
- A **trip ahead** is a vacation, waiting or approved, that doesn't cover the period but starts after its first day and reaches its last 7 days. Today, the pact page and Plan a vacation say "Do it by" the day before the trip. The window then starts 7 days before the trip and runs to the period's end, but no reminder goes off on the trip's own days, so one that ends before the period does reminds again once the owner is back (`periodTrip`, `tripAhead`, `tripAheadOf`, `trips.ts`). Test: `period-schedules.test.ts`, "the reminder window".
- An approved excuse for any day covers the whole period, as on Weekly count. A change applies from the next period; an end request and an end date end the pact once the last whole period up to the end date closes. Request change shows no Schedule or Starts row for them (`ChangePact.tsx`).

### Edit window

- For the first 10 minutes after creation the owner can edit or delete the pact freely (`EDIT_WINDOW_MS`, `pact-rules.ts`; `editPact`, `pacts.ts`; `deletePact`, `pacts.ts`). An edit does not restart the window. Test: `edit-window.test.ts`.
- An edit removes evidence for a day the new schedule does not have, or has but that has not opened yet: evidence sent for a One-time occurrence is not kept for a later Fixed day that falls on the old deadline (design review ticket 41). Test: `edit-window.test.ts`. An edit to a One-time deadline moves its occurrence with its evidence (`editPact`, `moveOneTimeOccurrence`, `pacts.ts`).
- **Known bug** ([ticket 48](../.scratch/design-review/issues/48-edit-leaves-a-satisfied-anki-day.md)): an edit leaves an Anki day already judged Satisfied by a check in the window, even on a day the new schedule no longer has; only days with evidence are removed.
- Reviewers are told they were picked only when the window closes, and evidence submitted inside the window goes to them then (`closeEditWindows`, `pacts.ts`). Until then a reviewer cannot see or decide the pact's evidence (`evidenceToReview`; `reviewable` in `evidenceKind`, `evidence.ts`).
- No excuse can be filed while the window is open (`edit-window-open`, `excuseOptions`, `excuses.ts`), and vacations offer only pacts past it (`offeredPacts`, `vacations.ts`).

### Change requests

- After the edit window, the owner changes a pact through `changePact` (`change-requests.ts`), which takes the whole terms from the Request change form. What needs approval is decided per term by `termDiffs` (`changes.ts`):
  - Name, reminders and added reviewers apply at once. They apply at once even when sent alongside terms that wait, and added reviewers are told they were picked, and the pact's other reviewers are told who was added (`addReviewersNow`, `change-requests.ts`; design review ticket 43). That includes a reviewer a waiting change request would remove; nobody is told while the pact's reviewers haven't yet been told of the pact. Test: `change-requests.test.ts`, "a pact's other reviewers are told who was added".
  - Description, deck, recipient and removing a reviewer always need approval. Why adding a reviewer needs none, though it lets the owner add someone lenient: `docs/adr/0004-adding-a-reviewer-needs-no-approval.md`.
  - Changes that relax the pact need approval: a lower penalty, a later due-by or bedtime, a later start, an earlier or added end date, fewer days, or a lower target. Changes that tighten it need no approval and apply from the first day not yet open, like an approved change (`STRICTER`, `changes.ts`).
- A change needs the Oura or AnkiWeb connection only to give an Anki pact another deck; with the connection gone, every other change is taken as usual (`evidenceSource`, `pacts.ts`). An edit in the edit window follows the same rule; creating an Oura Ring or Anki pact needs the connection. Test: `change-requests.test.ts`, "a change with the Oura or AnkiWeb connection gone".
- The schedule kind cannot change (`schedule-kind-fixed`, `checkedChange`, `change-requests.ts`). A start date cannot change once the pact has started (`pact-started`, `openOccurrencesRefusal`, `change-requests.ts`).
- The pact page's Name, Add a reviewer and Reminders sheets send only their own term, through `renamePact`, `addReviewers` (only the reviewers added) and `setReminders` (the whole list; the last one sent wins), each in `change-requests.ts`. Each applies to the pact's terms as they stand, so a page left open while a change was approved can't put the old terms back. They share `changePact`'s rules for their term (`checkedName`, `wellFormedReminders` and `remindersInTime` in `pact-rules.ts`; `mayReview`, `pacts.ts`) and are refused only for that term or when the pact isn't the owner's, has Ended or is in its edit window (`changeablePact`). The same name or reminders again, or a reviewer already reviewing, change nothing and aren't refused. Test: `change-requests.test.ts`, "the pact page's at-once changes apply to the terms as they stand".
- **Known bug** ([ticket 37](../.scratch/design-review/issues/37-request-change-sends-stale-terms.md)): the Request change form sends every term as it loaded them, so a term changed elsewhere since (an approved change request, say) is put back as part of the change.
- Only one change request waits at a time. While one waits, only at-once changes are accepted (`change-request-waiting`, `checkedChange`).
- Reminders apply at once and an approved change leaves them as they are, so each must go off before the due time in force now and before the one a change asks for: a change that makes the due-by time, the in-bed-by time or a One-time deadline earlier than a reminder is refused, and while a change request waits, so is a reminder at or after the due time it asks for, through `changePact` or `setReminders` (`reminder-after-due-time`, `remindersInTimeNow`, `change-requests.ts`; design review ticket 38). A reminder that is valid today can then be refused for a change that is later rejected; once the request is decided or withdrawn, it can be set. Test: `change-requests.test.ts`, "reminders go off before the due time a waiting change request asks for, too".
- An approved schedule change applies from the first day not yet open: tomorrow, or next Monday for Weekly count (`firstDayNotOpen`, `pact-rules.ts`). Change requests never rescue an occurrence that is open now. The earlier terms are kept per occurrence in `pact_earlier_terms` (`applyTerms`, `change-requests.ts`). Test: `change-requests.test.ts`.
- An end date approved too late to keep the day open now is moved to that day (`laterDate(after.endDate, lastOpen)` in `applyTerms`).
- With an end date, a Weekly count pact counts only whole weeks: it Ends after the last whole week before the end date (`pact-rules.ts`; decided in pact-v0 ticket 14). Test: `weekly-count.test.ts`.
- One-time: the new deadline must be at least an hour away, both when sent and when approved (`deadlineFarEnough` in `checkedChange`; `approvableNow`, `change-requests.ts`). A request decided after the occurrence was judged leaves the pact as it was (`applyOneTimeTerms`, `change-requests.ts`). Test: `one-time.test.ts`.
- Before approving a change request, the decider first catches judging up to now (`checkDecision` in `changeRequestKind`, `change-requests.ts`), so terms never apply to an occurrence already due and an end approved with nothing open leaves no due occurrence unjudged.
- A reviewer being removed can still decide until the request is approved, and is deleted at approval (`changeOutcome`, `change-requests.ts`; `decideChangeRequest`, `change-requests.ts`). The owner is told the outcome (`change-request-decided`).
- The owner can withdraw a waiting request. Nobody is notified, and its page says "You withdrew it" (`withdrawChangeRequest`, `change-requests.ts`).
- An end request is refused once the pact has Ended, while the edit window is open, or while another request waits (`requestEnd`, `change-requests.ts`).
- While an approved end waits for its open occurrence, the pact takes no new end request and no change beyond the at-once ones (the name, the reminders, added reviewers); both are refused with `end-approved` (`requestEnd`, `checkedChange`). Anything else would apply after the end, and a later end date, a stricter change, would undo it without approval. The pact page shows neither End pact nor Request change then, so the owner can't open the change form; the name, the reminders and added reviewers still change under Terms. Test: `change-requests.test.ts`, "while an approved end waits".
- An approved end request takes effect when the occurrence or week open at approval closes, as every other change does (rule R1). An occurrence is open from when it opens until it closes, unless it is judged; a bedtime night opens at the start of its date and closes at 18:00 the next day. Approval sets the end date to the last day of that occurrence's period (`endTiming`, `endAfter`, `ending.ts`, `:72`); a One-time pact keeps its deadline and Ends when it is judged. Until then the pact is Active, its open occurrence is judged normally and takes evidence and excuses as usual, and its page says "Ending approved. The pact ends after Sun Jan 18, once this week has been judged." With nothing open, the pact Ends at once. The owner's push says "Gym ends after Sun Jan 18", or "Gym has ended". The reviewer sees when it would End before approving. Test: `change-requests.test.ts`, "ending a pact".
- When a change takes days off the schedule (fewer days, a later start date, an earlier end date), the excuse and vacation days on those dates from the first day not open are deleted, unless the occurrence is judged (rule R9, `uncoverRemovedDays`, `covers.ts`, called from `applyTerms`). A later change that adds a day back finds it not covered, as vac:61 says, and it is judged as usual. A Weekly count target change removes no week. An excuse or vacation left with no days is moot, and is withdrawn as no longer needed (see "Withdrawn items" under Review items). One-time pacts move their covers with the deadline instead (`moveOneTimeOccurrence`, `pacts.ts`). Every table keyed by an occurrence must be handled in both places, or `scripts/check-conventions.ts` says why not. Test: `excuses.test.ts` and `vacations.test.ts`, "a change that removes a covered day".

### Ending

- A pact with an end date Ends at the start of the day after the end date, or when its last occurrence closes if that is later. Nobody is notified (`endPastEndDate`, `ending.ts`). An end date an approved end request set Ends it the same way. Test: `due-by-and-dates.test.ts`.
- A One-time pact Ends when its occurrence is judged (`settle`, `settle.ts`).
- An Ended pact's Missed days can still be excused for the rest of their 48 hours, as on an Active pact (see Excuses). Its page shows File excuse alone, where it sits on an Active pact, while a day's excuse window is open (`excuseWindowsClose` on the pact page, `excuseWindowsClose` in `excuses.ts`), and its last line reads "This pact ended on Sun Nov 22. You owe penalties for missed days unless an excuse is approved." Otherwise it reads "This pact ended on Sun Nov 22. You still owe penalties for days already missed." (`PactPage.tsx`). A sheet left open when the last window closes stays open with what was typed; sending it is then refused ("None of these days can be excused now."). Test: `excuses.test.ts`, "when the excuse windows of a pact's Missed days close"; `PactPage.test.tsx`, "File excuse on an Ended pact".
- Every ending goes through `endPact(ctx, pactId, cause, at)` (`ending.ts`), with cause `end-date`, `one-time-judged` or `end-request`. It withdraws a waiting change request, silently, recording `pact-ended`; its page says "Withdrawn because the pact ended" and a reviewer who tries to decide it is told so. Test: `change-requests.test.ts`, "a change request still waiting when the end date passes is withdrawn" and "an end request still waiting when the end date passes is withdrawn".
- Only `settle.ts`, `ending.ts` and migrations insert or delete penalties, update an occurrence's outcome or judging time, or update a pact's stage (`scripts/check-conventions.ts`).
- Only Active pacts are judged and reminded (`pactsToJudge`, `occurrence-state.ts`; `remind`, `reminders.ts`). An occurrence due after the end is ignored (`dueAfterEnd`, `occurrence-state.ts`).
- The pact list shows pacts in the edit window first, then Active, then Ended (`pactList`, `pacts.ts`).

## Occurrences and judging

### Days and due times

- A Camera day runs from midnight in the account's timezone to the due time (`fixedDaysOccurrence`, `occurrences.ts`; `dayClockOf`, `pact-rules.ts`).
- An Oura Ring night opens at the start of its date and is due at the in-bed-by time. A time before 18:00 means the next day. The night closes at 18:00 the next day. All of it is read on Oura's clock (`nightStartsAt`, `occurrences.ts`; `ouraClockOf`, `rows.ts`). Test: `oura.test.ts`.
- An Anki day opens at its rollover hour. Due-by is allowed from noon on day D, after the rollover hour, up to the rollover hour on D+1. Other times are refused (`ankiDueByDayAfter`, `occurrences.ts`). Anki days use the account's timezone, not the collection's (deviation from the anki spec, noted in anki ticket 03).
- A Weekly count week runs Monday to Sunday and is due at the end of Sunday (`periodOccurrence`, `occurrences.ts`).
- A One-time occurrence with Submit anytime opens at creation. Otherwise it opens at the start of the deadline's date (`oneTimeOccurrence`, `occurrences.ts`).

### Judging

- Every judging path goes through `settle` (`settle.ts`): the tick, evidence decisions, cover decisions and withdrawals, bedtime nights and Anki checks. It reads the occurrence's facts, asks `verdictOf` (`src/core/verdict.ts`) for its outcome, and stores it with its penalty if the occurrence isn't judged yet. A stored outcome is kept, except that a Missed one an approved cover now excuses becomes Excused without its penalty (design review ticket 12), and an Excused one that approved evidence now makes Satisfied becomes Satisfied (ticket 26).
- A penalty on a bill is never excused or changed: the excuse window closes before billing takes a penalty, and `settle` throws rather than touch a billed penalty, so the command changes nothing (`deletePenalties`, `settle.ts`). Test: `bills.test.ts` "a billed penalty".
- The tick settles each Active pact's occurrences in due order, and keeps a `judged_through` cursor (`settlePactDueBy`, `settle.ts`). After a move east the first day in the new timezone can be due before the last one in the old: the tick judges it at its due time, and the cursor waits before the older day (`dueAfter`, `occurrences.ts`). Test: `judging.test.ts`, `timezone.test.ts`.
- At the due time an occurrence is judged in this order:
  1. Approved evidence: Satisfied.
  2. An approved excuse or vacation: Excused.
  3. Evidence waiting for a reviewer: wait.
  4. Otherwise: Missed, with the penalty.
- A cover approved at the due instant itself, before the tick, judges the occurrence in the same order: approved evidence still makes it Satisfied, not Excused (`verdictOf`; design review ticket 31). Test: `verdict.test.ts`, "a cover approved at it"; `vacations.test.ts`, "approved at the due time itself".
- The owner gets a Missed push whenever an occurrence or week becomes Missed, whatever judged it: the due time or a late evidence decision (rule R4, design review ticket 11). Two exceptions: nobody is told about a day an excuse or vacation covers, waiting or approved (decided in pact-v0 ticket 07), and a rejection that judges a Fixed day or One-time occurrence Missed tells the owner through the rejection push alone (v0:324). A Missed push caused by a decision comes after the decision's push (`settle`). Test: `weekly-count.test.ts` "a week judged by a late decision", `rejection.test.ts`.
- A Weekly count week is Satisfied when its days of approved evidence reach the target. Otherwise an approved cover makes it Excused, waiting evidence holds it, and else it is Missed with one penalty of days short × penalty (`verdictOf`). Test: `weekly-count.test.ts`.
- The judging rules are stated once in `verdictOf` (`src/core/verdict.ts`); every scenario checks the stored outcomes against it (design review ticket 10).
- The penalty uses the terms that applied to the occurrence, including its recipient (`termsOn`, read by `occurrenceFacts`).
- A One-time occurrence with approved evidence is Satisfied at the deadline (decided in pact-v0 ticket 15).

### How an occurrence is shown

- Every screen that shows an occurrence (the heat map's squares and chip, Yesterday, the excuse sheet, the excuse page and the Excuse review item) words it through one function, `presentOccurrence` (`src/client/excuse-days.tsx`), in one order. Satisfied, Excused (Vacation when an approved vacation covers it) or Missed. A Missed day under a waiting cover reads "Missed · excuse waiting for approval" or "Missed · vacation waiting for approval". A day not judged yet reads, first match wins: Excused or Vacation under an approved cover; "Excuse waiting for approval" or "Vacation waiting for approval"; "Waiting for approval" while its evidence waits; then Coming up, Due today ("This week" for a Weekly count week; "Due Mon Jan 12" for a One-time pact open before its date) or Not judged yet ("No Oura data" for a bedtime night). Test: `excuse-days.test.tsx`.
- A heat map square's colour is its outcome, Vacation, or for a day not judged yet whether it is ahead, open or past due. Its label is the chip's words, so the square and the chip agree. Test: `history.test.tsx`.
- A One-time pact's History is its one square beside the date and chip. The date stays on one line; a chip too long to fit beside it ("Missed · excuse waiting for approval") wraps below (`.hmdetail.one`, `src/client/styles.css`).
- The browser clock only moves a day not judged yet from Coming up to Due today to Not judged yet while a screen is open; the core decides everything else.

### Evidence

- Camera only. One to 10 photos (`MAX_EVIDENCE_PHOTOS`), for the occurrence open now and before its due time. The received time is the latest photo's receipt (`submitEvidence`, `evidence.ts`; `evidenceAcceptance`, `evidence.ts`). Test: `evidence.test.ts`.
- One piece of evidence per day may wait or be approved. On a Weekly count, a day with rejected evidence takes no more (decided in pact-v0 ticket 14). Evidence is refused once the occurrence is judged or the pact has Ended.
- A rejection judges the occurrence Missed at once, unless the day was excused ahead, which leaves it Excused (`decideEvidence`, `evidence.ts`; decided in pact-v0 ticket 07). Test: `rejection.test.ts`.
- An approval judges the occurrence Satisfied at once if it is past its due time. A late decision on a Weekly count week judges the week at once: Satisfied if its approved days reach the target, else Missed, with the Missed push.
- An approval of evidence that was still waiting when an approved excuse or vacation made the occurrence Excused makes it Satisfied as of the approval, with no penalty; a Weekly count week once its approved days reach the target (vac:78; `settle`, `settle.ts`; design review ticket 26). A One-time pact stays Ended as of the Excused judging. A rejection leaves it Excused. Test: `evidence.test.ts`, "a late approval on an Excused occurrence".
- The owner is told each decision (`evidence-decided`). A rejection shows an alert until the owner takes the L or files an excuse (`rejectedEvidenceAlerts`, `evidence.ts`). On a Weekly count, the rejected-day alert appears when the week is judged (`rejectedDayAlerts`, `evidence.ts`).
- Take the L accepts a miss and clears its alert (`acceptMiss`, `evidence.ts`). Test: `accept-miss.test.ts`.

### Oura Ring

- The first long sleep that starts in the night window decides the night: Satisfied if it starts by the in-bed-by time, Missed if later. No sleep by the time the night closes means Missed at close. The first long sleep is stored on the night as it arrives, with when it arrived, and the night is settled from it (`judgeNights`, `bedtime.ts`). Test: `bedtime.test.ts`.
- An approved excuse beats a late sleep. A sleep on time beats an excuse (`verdictOf`; decided in pact-v0 ticket 08).
- A night closes on Oura's clock where the owner was when it opened: the UTC offset of the latest sleep Oura had reported, or the account's timezone when that offset is the timezone's own (`ouraOffsetOf`, `rows.ts`). When Oura reports a sleep on another clock, every night that has opened keeps the clock it had, as a day keeps its timezone (design review ticket 45): `noteOuraClock` (`bedtime.ts`) keeps the old clock for the nights dated up to the latest whose window has started on either clock, or up to the last day open in a timezone the owner moved from (`oura_earlier_clock`, `keepEarlierClock`), and the new clock applies from the next night. So a night judged Missed at its close keeps that close, and an open night gains or loses no hours; a sleep still counts for the night its own timestamp falls in (`nightOf`, `oura-clock.ts`). Test: `bedtime.test.ts`, "a night judged at its close keeps that close when Oura later reports a sleep on another clock" and "a night open when Oura reports another clock keeps the clock it opened on".
- If no Oura data has come in by 15:00 the next day, the owner gets one push for that night and a No Oura data card, unless the night is no longer something to do (`noOuraDataPushAt`, `occurrences.ts`; `pushNoOuraData`, `bedtime.ts`). Test: `oura-no-data.test.ts`.
- Oura is asked at most every 15 seconds when the owner checks now (`checkOura`, `bedtime.ts`). A failed fetch is logged and retried next tick and affects only that account. A check now whose fetch failed answers that Oura couldn't be reached, unless the failure was Oura refusing Pact's app, which the operator is already told of (`OperatorAlerted`, `ports.ts`); one that finds the connection revoked, or nothing to check, answers ok. A check skipped within 15 seconds of the last answers what that one found, waiting for it if it is still running, so a quick second pull can't hide a failure (`recentCheckNow`, `connections.ts`; `CheckNowResult`, `src/shared/types.ts`). Test: `oura-no-data.test.ts`, "checking Oura now". A revoked token marks the connection Needs reconnecting and stops calls for that account (decided in pact-v0 tickets 08 and 09). Disconnecting deletes the tokens.
- Only an `invalid_grant` answer from Oura's token endpoint means the account's code or refresh token is no good, so a refresh refused that way marks the connection Needs reconnecting. Any other 400 or 401 there (such as `invalid_client` from a wrong `OURA_CLIENT_SECRET`, or one with no readable error) means Pact's app is set up wrong: the account keeps its tokens and is tried again next tick as for a failed fetch, the refresh is not marked as maybe spent, and an operator alert names the error and says to check `OURA_CLIENT_ID`, `OURA_CLIENT_SECRET` and the token URL (`invalid_request` usually means the wrong token endpoint). A connect refused that way shows the owner the failed result ("Oura didn't connect. Try again.") and raises the alert too (`#tokenRequest`, `oura.ts`; pact-v0 ticket 21). Test: `oura.test.ts`, "a refresh Oura refuses for Pact's app" and "a connect Oura refuses for Pact's app".

### Anki

- An Anki day is Satisfied at the first check showing 0 new, 0 learning and 0 review cards left (`recordAnkiChecks`, `anki-days.ts`, which settles the day). Test: `anki-days.test.ts`.
- Days still to do are polled every hour, and every 10 minutes in the last hour before the due time, since only a check before the due time can satisfy a day. A tick that sends a reminder for a day still to do checks AnkiWeb first, so a deck finished since the last check stops the reminder (`ankiPactsToRemind`, `reminders.ts`). The owner can check now at most every 15 seconds; Today does so when it opens and whenever the app comes back to the front while an Anki day is to do, on a pull to refresh, and when Today refreshes by itself on coming back after a while or at midnight (see Coming back to the front), and a check now restarts the hour (`ANKI_POLL_INTERVAL_MS`, `ANKI_LAST_HOUR_POLL_INTERVAL_MS`, `fetchAnki`, `anki-days.ts`; `ankiDaysWaiting`, `anki-days.ts`). Test: `anki-days.test.ts`, "checking AnkiWeb".
- A check now answers that AnkiWeb couldn't be reached when its call failed (a timeout, a network failure, the check script failing), unless it was a protocol error, which the operator is already told of (`OperatorAlerted`, `ports.ts`). One that finds the login refused, or no day waiting, answers ok; one skipped within 15 seconds of the last answers what that one found, waiting for it if it is still running (`checkAnki`, `anki-days.ts`). Test: `anki-days.test.ts`, "checking AnkiWeb".
- A check still running at the due time holds judging for up to 3 minutes (`ANKI_CHECK_HOLDS_JUDGING_MS`, `judging.ts`).
- Losing the connection marks it Needs reconnecting (`markDisconnected`, `connections.ts`). A deck that no longer exists raises an alert (`anki_pact_deck`).
- Pact keeps one copy of each owner's collection, downloaded from AnkiWeb without media only when AnkiWeb says it changed (`fresh_copy`, `anki_check.py`). After each download the note fields are blanked, so the copy holds no note content (`prune`). The rest stays: note rows (ids, tags), cards, notetypes, decks with their descriptions, deck options, config and the review log. A copy downloaded before pruning was added is pruned at its next download; there is no migration. The collection's change stamp (`mod`, `scm`, `usn`, `ls`) is checked on disk after pruning; if pruning moved it (the anki package's behaviour changed), it is put back, so the next check doesn't download again, and the check succeeds with a warning, raised as an operator alert. The script never uploads: the pruned copy would wipe the owner's notes on AnkiWeb (`download`). Disconnecting deletes the copy. Each successful deck list or counts check logs one `anki-fetch` line: the op, the account, whether it reused the copy or downloaded (with no copy yet, or because AnkiWeb reported changes), AnkiWeb's sync status `required` value, the copy's size as downloaded and as pruned, and the seconds the change check, download and prune took (`fetchLine`, `anki.ts`). Test: `anki-check.test.ts`, `anki.test.ts`.

## Covers: excuses, vacations and holds

### Excuses

- An excuse covers one pact for a range of days and needs a reason, at most 3 photos (`MAX_EXCUSE_PHOTOS`) and at most 62 days (`MAX_EXCUSE_SPAN_DAYS`) (`fileExcuse`, `excuses.ts`; `excusePreview`, `excuses.ts`). Test: `excuses.test.ts`.
- It can cover future days, today, and past days that are still waiting on evidence or were Missed with their excuse window still open (`excuseOptions`, `excuses.ts`; `EXCUSE_WINDOW_MS`, `pact-rules.ts`). The window is 48 hours from the later of the day's judging and the rejection or withdrawal of a vacation that covered it (rule R3 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`).
- The pact's stage doesn't matter: an Ended pact's Missed day can be excused while its window is open, and approval makes it Excused and deletes its penalty as on an Active pact. Take the L only takes the day off Today; it stays excusable from the pact page for the rest of its window. Test: `one-time.test.ts`, "a Missed occurrence can be excused within 48 hours".
- File excuse on the pact page offers the days dated today or earlier that an excuse would cover now, and a Missed day dated later whose window is open: a rejection can judge a One-time occurrence Missed before its date, which Ends the pact (`excuseOptions`, `recentDays`, `excuses.ts`). Test: `excuses.test.ts`, "a One-time occurrence a rejection judged Missed before its date is offered, dated ahead".
- The pact page's `excuseWindowsClose` lists when the excuse windows of the Missed days `excuseOptions` offers close, soonest first, so the page and the sheet agree (`excuseWindowsClose`, `excuses.ts`). On an Ended pact the button counts down to the soonest still to come, like Today's Missed card ("File excuse · 31h left", `fileExcuseLabel`, `src/client/format.ts`): when that window closes it moves to the next without a reload, and once that day is excused the reloaded page drops it. An Active pact's File excuse has no countdown. Test: `excuses.test.ts`, "when the excuse windows of a pact's Missed days close"; `PactPage.test.tsx`, "once the soonest window closes, it counts down to the next".
- The excuse sheet from an Active pact's page asks "Which day?": a recent day or Days ahead. An Ended pact has no days ahead (`excuseOptions` says so with `daysAhead`): with one day to excuse the sheet opens on that day as Today's Missed card does, without "+ Later days too"; with several it asks which, without Days ahead (`fromPactPage`, `src/client/ExcuseSheet.tsx`). The footer says "this day" when the excuse covers one day ("You won't owe the $10 for this day unless the excuse is rejected."), else "these days"; on a Weekly count, weeks ("Covers 1 week. … for this week …"). Test: `ExcuseSheet.test.tsx`.
- Days after the end, days already excused, and days on a waiting vacation are skipped (`excuseSkipReason`, `excuses.ts`). Days a change removed from the schedule are not offered (`excuseDays`, `excuses.ts`).
- On a Weekly count, excusing any day of a week covers the whole week (`excusePreview`, `excuses.ts`). Test: `weekly-count.test.ts`.
- An excuse dated any day of a Submit anytime window covers the One-time occurrence (decided in pact-v0 ticket 15).
- The pact's reviewers get a push. Any one of them decides it (`decideExcuse`, `excuses.ts`).
- Approval turns Missed days into Excused and deletes their penalties, and judges days past their due time as Excused now. Oura nights not judged yet are left to judging, which waits for Oura until they close. Rejection and withdrawal settle the days too, which changes nothing, and a check of AnkiWeb still running holds an Anki day as it does for the tick (`settleCoverDays`, `settle.ts`).
- After a rejection the owner can file again while a day is still excusable, or take the L. The alert goes once no day is left (`rejectedExcuseAlerts`, `excuses.ts`; `acceptExcuseRejection`, `excuses.ts`). A rejected excuse never restarts a day's window (v0:256; `excuses.test.ts` "re-filing after a rejection").
- The owner cannot withdraw an excuse; the system withdraws a moot one (see "Withdrawn items").

### Vacations

- A vacation covers several pacts at once, for dates after today, at most 62 days. The owner picks the reviewers who decide it from the pacts' reviewers (`vacationPreview`, `vacations.ts`; `fileVacation`, `vacations.ts`). Test: `vacations.test.ts`.
- It offers Active pacts past their edit window. A One-time pact whose occurrence is already open is listed but cannot be covered (`offeredPacts`, `vacations.ts`; `vacationSkipReason`, `vacations.ts`).
- A Weekly count week is covered only when the vacation covers its Thursday, the middle of its Mon–Sun, so a vacation starting on a Sunday doesn't take the week before. Two weeks are covered only when it covers their whole second week, and a month only from its 15th to its last day; one it doesn't cover may be a trip ahead (`coveringDays`, `vacations.ts`). The Check step lists each week it does not cover (`pactPreview`, `vacations.ts`; `PlanVacation.tsx`). This differs on purpose from an excuse (rule R11 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`).
- Only its deciders are told, see it in their Review Queue, get the owner's replies and may decide it: the picked reviewers who still review a covered pact, or with none left, any current reviewer of a covered pact (rule R5, design review ticket 19). A picked reviewer removed from every covered pact loses it; a reviewer added while a picked one remains does not get it. When an approved change removes the last picked reviewer, each new decider gets the usual review-item push for the vacation, once; a reviewer added to a covered pact once no picked reviewer remains sees it in the queue without a push. The Profile card's "Sent to", the owner's reply message ("Sam can decide it now.") and the review item name the current deciders, not the reviewers picked at filing (design review ticket 30) (`vacationDeciders`, `tellNewVacationDeciders`, `vacations.ts`). Test: `vacations.test.ts`, "who decides a vacation when a covered pact's reviewers change". Approval excuses each covered day as an approved excuse would (`decideVacation`, `vacations.ts`). When it is approved, every other reviewer of a pact with a covered day is told, with the dates, the pacts they review and who approved it (`decideVacation`, `tellOtherReviewers`, `vacations.ts`; design review ticket 44). The approver is not told; a picked decider who did not decide is. A filing, a rejection or a withdrawal tells nobody else, a ticked pact with no covered day is not named, and a reviewer added after the approval is not told. Test: `vacations.test.ts`, "the other reviewers of covered pacts hear of an approval".
- The owner can withdraw a vacation that waits. Nobody is notified (`withdrawVacation`, `vacations.ts`). A vacation the system withdraws as moot leaves Profile as one the owner withdrew does: a vacation has no page of its own to show the reason.
- Rejecting or withdrawing a vacation reopens the excuse window of each covered day already Missed: 48 hours from the rejection or withdrawal (rule R3). Those days show on Today's Missed for the rest of it, beside the rejection alert. The rejection push and the alert say which days can be excused and until when (the alert "Mon Jan 12 to Wed Jan 14 can be excused until Sat Jan 17, 8:00am.", the push "You can file an excuse for Mon Jan 12 to Wed Jan 14 until Sat Jan 17, 8:00am."), and say nothing of excuses when no day can be (`releasedDays`, `decideVacation`, `rejectedVacationAlerts` in `vacations.ts`). Profile's withdraw confirm says the held days can be excused for 48 hours. This reverses vac:81 for vacations only (design review ticket 15). Test: `vacations.test.ts`, "the excuse window a rejected or withdrawn vacation reopens".
- After a rejection, Take the L covers only the days that were Missed at the time of the rejection (`acceptVacationRejection`, `vacations.ts`; decided in vacation ticket 03).
- Moving a One-time deadline keeps a vacation day only if the new deadline is inside the vacation (`moveOneTimeOccurrence`, `pacts.ts`; `moveCovers`, `covers.ts`).

### Holds and the 48-hour window

- A waiting excuse or vacation that covers a Missed day holds its penalty: it is left off bills and out of what is owed (`PENALTY_HELD`, `money.ts`). Test: `ledgers.test.ts`.
- A bill takes only penalties whose excuse window closed by its time, so a Missed day can still be excused before it is billed (`billOwner`, `money.ts`). The window opens at the later of the day's judging and the rejection or withdrawal of a vacation that covered it, except that nothing reopens it once the penalty is on a bill (bills made before R3 could hold one); one expression decides it everywhere it is read: the occurrence view, excuse filing, Today's Missed cards and alerts, the ledger's next bill, and bills (`excuseWindowOpensSql` and `excusableUntil`, `covers.ts`; rule R3).
- Excuses and vacations are read as one kind of cover (`covers.ts`; the `cover_day_v` view). When one day has several covers, a waiting excuse comes first, then an approved excuse, then an approved vacation, then a waiting vacation (`coverOn`, `covers.ts`). A rejected or withdrawn cover covers nothing.
- A waiting excuse takes the day off the owner's to-do list and stops its reminders. A waiting vacation keeps both (`keepsToDo`, `covers.ts`; rule R7 below).

## Review items

The five kinds are evidence, excuses, vacations, payments and change requests.

- Who decides: each kind's handler's `deciders` (`itemToDecide`, `review.ts`; the handlers are listed in `REQUEST_KINDS`, `request-kinds.ts`):
  - Evidence and excuses: the pact's current reviewers.
  - Payments: anyone who reviews one of the owner's pacts with that recipient, now, in earlier terms, or through a penalty to it (`paymentReviewersSql`, `money.ts`).
  - Vacations: the reviewers picked at filing who still review at least one of its pacts; when none are left, every current reviewer of one of its pacts (`vacationDeciders`, `vacations.ts`; rule R5 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`). This changes vac:69 and vac:70, which fixed the picked reviewers at filing.
  - Change requests: the pact's reviewers, while the request is not withdrawn and the pact is Active.
- The first decision is final. Others get `already-decided`. A rejection needs a reason (`decide`, `review.ts`). Test: `review.test.ts`.
- A reviewer can ask the owner a question. Only the owner is notified, and the item leaves the review queue until the owner replies. The reply notifies every reviewer (`askOwner`, `review.ts`; `ownerReply`, `review.ts`; decided in pact-v0 ticket 12). Test: `reviewer-questions.test.ts`.
- The review queue shows change requests first, then everything else oldest first (`queuedItems`, `review.ts`; `queueFirst` on `changeRequestKind`).
- No item decides itself, and none expires (rule R2 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`, design review ticket 21). Instead, at 09:00 in the recipient's timezone, once a local day at most and never within 12 hours of the last, so a short move west doesn't bring a second (after a move west of more than 12 hours the next 09:00 can carry the same date again) (`remindStale`, `stale-items.ts`, run in the tick; `stale_notice` keeps it to one a day across a restart). Test: `stale-items.test.ts`, "no second one after a move west":
  - A reviewer is pushed while an item in their Review Queue has waited more than 24 hours by that 09:00: "3 items are waiting for your review", "The oldest, Jordan's evidence for Gym, has waited 2 days.", opening Review. The count is the whole Review Queue. An item waits from when it was sent for a decision, or from the owner's latest reply if later, which sent it back (`reviewQueueWaits`, `review.ts`). Items waiting on the owner's reply aren't counted. Days are calendar days in the reviewer's timezone. It follows the "Something to review" setting.
  - The owner is pushed while a reviewer's question to them has gone unanswered more than 24 hours by that 09:00, naming the oldest: "Sam asked about your excuse for Gym 2 days ago", "Reply so Sam can decide.", opening the item's own page. It follows the "Questions and replies" setting.
  - So evidence filed Monday 10:00 and not decided is first reminded Wednesday 09:00, then daily until it's decided. A payment that covers the whole overdue amount and is never decided is reminded this way too, since it is in the reviewer's queue. Test: `stale-items.test.ts`.
  - Future idea, not planned: an item decided by default after N days. Rejected for now because between two friends either default is wrong: automatic approval is exploitable and automatic rejection is unfair.
- Withdrawal: an owner can withdraw a change request or a vacation, not evidence, an excuse or a payment.
- Withdrawn items (rule R8 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`, design review ticket 20): when deciding a waiting item either way can no longer change any outcome, penalty or terms, the system withdraws it and records why, after every command and tick (`withdrawMoot`, `review.ts`, run in the core's `#command`). Evidence, an excuse or a vacation is moot when `verdictOf`, judged with it approved now and with it rejected now, gives every occurrence it touches the same final verdict and penalty (not open, nor waiting on something else), or it touches none any more. Evidence is judged approved together with the occurrence's other waiting evidence, so pieces of a Weekly count week that reach the target only together all stay, and evidence that couldn't reach the target even with every waiting piece approved is withdrawn (design review ticket 32) (`decisionEffect`, `verdict.ts`; `mootReason`, `moot.ts`). For a vacation that means no covered day left on any pact. A change request is moot once its pact has Ended. A payment never is: approving one always changes the ledger. Reasons: `pact-ended` when the occurrences are past the pact's end (or gone with an approved end), else `no-longer-needed`; the owner's own withdrawal records `owner`. Cases:
  - Withdrawn: an excuse or vacation whose every day a change removed or an approved end left past the pact's end; one whose every covered day approved evidence made Satisfied (once the day is judged); evidence for a Weekly count week its approved days already Satisfied (once the week is judged); a change request when its pact Ends.
  - Kept: evidence for a day an approved cover already made Excused, since approving it makes the day Satisfied (vac:78), and on a Weekly count week judged Excused, every waiting piece while approving all of them would reach the target; evidence or a cover on an occurrence still open, deferred on that evidence, or Missed; a payment; a change request on an Active pact.
  - A withdrawn item leaves every Review Queue, the list waiting on a reply, the owner's waiting list, and Today's question alerts; the owner can no longer reply to it. Nobody is pushed. Withdrawn evidence no longer counts as waiting, so it holds up no judging; a withdrawn excuse covers nothing. Withdrawing a moot excuse or vacation releases nothing, since it holds no penalty (`assertHoldsNoPenalty`, `moot.ts`).
  - The owner's evidence, excuse and change request pages say "Withdrawn because the pact ended", "Withdrawn because it was no longer needed" or "You withdrew it", with the date (`StateLine`, `src/client/item-page.tsx`). An excuse left with no day of the pact's (all removed by a change, or past an approved end) shows neither its dates nor its Days list (`ExcusePage.test.tsx`). A reviewer who tries to decide a withdrawn item gets `withdrawn` with the reason, and the Review tab says so ("Withdrawn because Gym has ended.", `refusalText`, `Review.tsx`).
  - Test: `withdrawal.test.ts`; `Review.test.tsx`, "approving an item the system withdrew says why it was withdrawn".
- Waiting lists: the owner has one waiting list, built over the registry for all five kinds (`waitingFor`, `src/core/waiting.ts`). Today's Waiting for approval shows all of it, newest first. The pact page's Waiting on shows the part that concerns the pact: its change request, evidence and excuses, and waiting vacations covering a day of it; never payments. Each row is the same everywhere (`WaitingItemRow`, `src/client/item-page.tsx`) and opens the item's own page (`openItem`): evidence its evidence page, an excuse, a payment or a change request its page, and a vacation the Profile sheet, where its card is (a vacation has no page of its own). A reviewer's question about any waiting item, a vacation's included, shows on Today as an alert whose Reply opens the same page (`questionAlerts`, `today.ts`); a vacation's Profile card keeps its Reply too. This overrides vac:71, vac:104 and vac:145, which kept waiting vacations and their questions off Today (rule R10 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`, design review ticket 18). Test: `vacations.test.ts`, "a waiting vacation in the owner's waiting list" and "a question about a vacation"; `Today.test.tsx` and `PactPage.test.tsx`.

## Money

- A penalty is owed to its recipient. Owed is unheld penalties less approved payments, never below zero (`ledgerSummaries`, `money.ts`).
- Approved payments beyond the unheld penalties are the ledger's credit, which arises when a paid penalty is then excused. The ledger card shows it beside owed $0 ("$5 credit. It pays your next penalties.") until the next penalties use it up; Today shows nothing for it. While a waiting excuse or vacation holds a penalty the credit would pay if rejected, the card says the credit lasts only if that is approved ("$5 credit if the excuse is approved"). Allocation and billing are unchanged: the credit pays the next penalties through `allocate` (`creditCents` and `creditWaitsOn`, `ledgerSummaries`, `money.ts`; rule R12 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`). Test: `ledgers.test.ts`, `Ledgers.test.tsx`.
- Bills are made each Wednesday at 18:00 in the owner's timezone, one per recipient, due 7 days later (`makeBills`, `money.ts`; `BILL_TERM_DAYS`, `bill-math.ts`). The owner is told only if something on it is unpaid. A timezone change carries billing on from the last Wednesday billed, in the new timezone: a move west doesn't bill that Wednesday again, and a move east past a Wednesday's 18:00 bills it at the next tick (`setTimezone`, `accounts.ts`; `lastBillDay`, `bill-math.ts`). Test: `bills.test.ts`.
- A payment needs a screenshot and may not exceed what is owed less payments already waiting (`recordPayment`, `money.ts`; `paymentAcceptance`, `money.ts`). Test: `payments.test.ts`.
- Approved payments go to billed penalties first, oldest bill first, then to unbilled ones by when they were added. Held penalties are skipped (`allocate`, `money.ts`).
- An Overdue spell starts when a bill is past due and unpaid. The owner is reminded daily. Reviewers are told when the spell starts and weekly after (`remindOverdue`, `money.ts`; `overdueOf`, `bill-math.ts`). The spell ends only when approved payments have paid every bill past due: paying off its first bill while a later one is past due leaves it running, so the reminders keep their times and the alert its reviewers (`joinedSpell`, `billsOf`, `money.ts`; design review ticket 46). Test: `bills.test.ts`, "paying off the oldest of two overdue bills doesn't end the spell".
- A payment waiting for approval stops Overdue reminders only for the part of the overdue amount it covers (rule R6 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`; `overdueOf`, `bill-math.ts`): a $1 payment waiting against $15 overdue leaves the daily reminder running for $14 (`bills.test.ts`, "F6"). One covering the whole amount stops them, and its reviewers get the daily stale-item reminder instead (see Review items).
- The overdue alert names reviewers only once they have been told in this spell (`billsForToday`, `money.ts`; decided in pact-v0 ticket 19).

## Notifications and reminders

- Each push belongs to one setting the person can turn off: picked as reviewer, review item, decision, comment, missed occurrence, new bill, overdue bill, connection problem, pacts you review (`NOTIFICATION_SETTINGS`, `src/shared/types.ts`; `SETTING_OF_KIND`, `outbox.ts`). The daily stale-item reminders have no setting of their own: a reviewer's follows review item, an owner's follows comment. The daily summary email has its own (below). Test: `notifications.test.ts`, `stale-items.test.ts`.
- Pushes are written to an outbox and sent after the change commits.
- The daily summary is an email a person turns on in Profile (Email section); it starts off and is apart from the push settings, which don't stop it, nor it them (`setDailySummary`, `accounts.ts`; `account.daily_summary_on_at`, when it was turned on). At 09:00 in the account's timezone, as the stale-item pushes, it lists every item in their Review Queue that had waited more than 24 hours by then, and every reviewer's question to them unanswered that long, each oldest first: who, what the item is, the pact (a payment's recipient, a vacation's dates) and how long, with no photos or free text, and one link per section (Review, Today) and a footer linking to Profile. One email with both, at most one a local day and none within 12 hours of the last, restart or timezone change or not (`stale_notice.summary_sent_at`; the stale-item pushes too), and none when both lists are empty (`remindStale`, `stale-items.ts`; the words in `summaryEmail`, `summary-email.ts`). The first goes at the first 09:00 after it was turned on: turned on at 08:00, that day's; at 22:00, the next day's. It goes to the account's Google sign-in email through the outbox as an `'email'` row, sent by the `Mailer` apart from the pushes: only the tick starts an email pass, it doesn't wait for it, and one runs at a time, so a slow or hung mail server holds up no push, command or tick; shutdown waits a few seconds for a send in flight (`deliverEmails`, `outbox.ts`). A failed send is retried at each tick, never after a command. Right before each send it's claimed, and only while it's less than 24 hours old and not given up; 24 hours after it was queued, unless it's being sent right then, it's given up, never tried again, even after a restart days later, and raised as an operator alert by log line and push, not by email, naming the person and the summary's date: "The Pact Daily Summary for Wed Nov 11 to Sam (sam@gmail.com) failed for 24 hours and was given up." (`giveUpEmails`, `alertGivenUpEmails`, `outbox.ts`). Test: `daily-summary.test.ts`.
- Operator alerts (reminders dropped after downtime, the database restored on boot, an Oura refresh token refused after a lost answer, Oura refusing Pact's app at its token endpoint, an AnkiWeb protocol error, an Anki check warning) each write an `OPERATOR_ALERT` log line and email the `OPERATOR_EMAIL` address at once, as plain text: subject "Pact operator alert: <title>", body the log line's words. The email skips the outbox and the database, so it still goes after a restore from an old backup; nothing waits for it (shutdown, for a few seconds), and a failed one is logged, not retried. An AnkiWeb protocol error is logged every time but emailed once per account until AnkiWeb answers that account again (`#protocolErrorEmailed`, `anki.ts`; in memory, so a restart may email once more). Oura refusing Pact's app is the same, but once for all accounts until Oura accepts Pact's app again, with tokens or an `invalid_grant` (`#appRefusalEmailed`, `oura.ts`). A warning in a successful Anki check (the copy's change stamp put back after pruning) raises an "Anki check warning" alert, logged every time and emailed once per account while the server runs (`#warningEmailed`, `anki.ts`). The first two also push to the operator's account through the outbox; the Oura and Anki alerts don't push. Nobody can turn them off (`operatorAlerts`, `operator-alerts.ts`). Production sends from the Gmail account in `GMAIL_ADDRESS` (`gmailMailer`, `gmail.ts`); development without one logs each email instead (`devMailer`). Test: `restore-alert.test.ts`, `reminders.test.ts` "after downtime", `oura.test.ts`, `anki.test.ts`, `gmail.test.ts`.
- Reminders go at their times in the account's timezone, once each (`remind`, `reminders.ts`; `reminder_sent`). One more than 15 minutes late is dropped with an operator alert (`REMINDER_LATE_MS`, `reminders.ts`). Test: `reminders.test.ts`.
- A reminder fires for an occurrence if and only if Today shows it as the owner's to do (a To do card, or for a bedtime night the Automatic or No Oura data card), as far as cover, judging and evidence go: one view decides both (`toDo` and `remind`, `occurrenceView`, `occurrence-state.ts`; rule R7 in `.scratch/design-review/spec.md`). An approved cover or a waiting excuse removes both; a waiting vacation keeps both. Reminders keep their own timing: each goes at its time, before the due time. Otherwise (`worthReminding`, `reminders.ts`):
  - Oura Ring: sent every scheduled night until Oura has judged it. The ring usually syncs the next morning, so most nights are reminded even when the owner is already in bed; a night judged before its reminder gets none (ticket 24 in `.scratch/design-review/issues/`).
  - Anki: sent until the day is judged. The tick checks AnkiWeb just before sending one (see Anki).
  - Camera: sent while no evidence is in for the occurrence and it is not judged.
  - Weekly count: sent on a day with no evidence, while approved and waiting days are fewer than the target. Test: `reminders.test.ts`.
  - Every 2 weeks and Once a month: sent on a day with no evidence in the period's reminder window (see "Every 2 weeks and Once a month"). Test: `period-schedules.test.ts`.
- Today shows alerts with connection problems first, then bills, then the rest newest first (`todayAlerts`, `today.ts`). Its sections are No Oura data, To do (bills included, by due time), the later section for Every 2 weeks and Once a month periods before their reminder window, Missed, Automatic and Coming up (`todaySections`, `today.ts`). Missed shows days whose excuse window is still open (rule R3), without misses from a rejection, days on a waiting cover, or days on a rejected excuse while its alert stands, whether the day was judged before or after the rejection: the alert files another excuse for them (`recentlyMissed`, `today.ts`; `rejectedExcuseAlertStandsSql`, `excuses.ts`). Test: `excuses.test.ts` "a rejected excuse with days not judged yet".
- Invites are single use and last 7 days. Your own invite, or one from someone already a friend, is refused (`INVITE_LIFETIME_MS`, `accounts.ts`). Test: `invites.test.ts`.

## Text and request limits

- Free text is capped, counted after trimming: a pact's or a recipient's name at 80 characters (`MAX_NAME_CHARS`), and every other text at 2,000 (`MAX_TEXT_CHARS`, both in `src/shared/types.ts`): a pact's description, change request and end request notes, excuse reasons, vacation and payment notes, reject reasons, questions and replies. Longer text is refused with `text-too-long` (`text-limits.ts`). The client's text fields stop at the same caps (`maxLength`). Test: `text-limits.test.ts`.
- An /api request body over 64 KB is refused with HTTP 413 (`body-too-large`) before it is read. The three photo upload routes (evidence, excuses, payments) have their own larger limits (`MAX_JSON_BODY_BYTES`, `postPhotos`, `src/server/app.ts`). Test: `app.test.ts`, "a JSON body over 64 KB".
- Text stored before these caps is left as it is.

## Time and timezones

- Everything runs on the account's timezone except Oura Ring nights, which use the offset Oura reports, each night the one it opened on (`ouraClockOf`, `rows.ts`; `noteOuraClock`, `bedtime.ts`; see Oura Ring). Test: `timezone.test.ts`.
- A timezone change applies only to occurrences that have not opened (design review ticket 39). `setTimezone` (`accounts.ts`) keeps the old timezone for each of the owner's pacts until the day after today there, or after today in the new timezone if that is later, so no day opens before the move (`pact_earlier_timezone`); a second change the same day keeps the first one's date, and a change to the same timezone records nothing. Each occurrence is worked out in the first earlier timezone its date is before, else the current one (`occurrencesOverTime`, `occurrences.ts`; `occurrencesOf`, `rows.ts`). So a day open or past keeps its due time, its excuse window and how it was judged: a Missed day stays Missed after a move west, and an open day gains or loses no hours. A Weekly count week goes by its Monday, so an open week keeps its end and its days. A One-time occurrence that has opened keeps its timezone (with Submit anytime, the one it was made in); one not open yet moves. Oura nights keep their own clock rule, on the timezone the night opened in.
- After a move east, the last day in the old timezone and the first in the new one are open together for some hours; the older one is the open one (for evidence and Today) until it is due (`openAt`, `occurrences.ts`). After a move west, the day still open in the old timezone may be tomorrow in the new one: a change or approved change request applies only from the day after it (`lastDayOpened`, `rows.ts`).
- When a timezone change makes due times later, each reviewer of the owner's Active Camera and Anki pacts is told once, with both timezones, the pacts they review and the day it starts (`setTimezone`, `tellReviewersOfLaterDueTimes`, `accounts.ts`; design review ticket 42). It needs no approval. A change that makes them earlier tells nobody. The shift is read at the start of the first day in the new timezone; pacts in their edit window, Oura Ring pacts, pacts with an end date before that day, and One-time pacts whose occurrence has opened (or that are Submit anytime) are left out. Profile says so under the timezone picker. Test: `timezone.test.ts`, "a timezone change and the owner's reviewers".
- Everything that is not an occurrence follows the current timezone at once: reminders' times, bills, the 09:00 pushes and the daily summary, and what today is on screens and for vacations. Test: `timezone.test.ts`, "a timezone change and the owner's days".
- Every account has a timezone. A new account gets its browser's timezone at sign-up: the client sends it through Google's redirect in the OAuth state (`TIMEZONE_SIGN_IN_KEY`, `src/shared/api.ts`), and `signIn` stores its canonical name (`accounts.ts`). A sign-in with no timezone, or one `Intl` does not accept, gets UTC; it is never refused. Signing in again leaves the timezone alone. The person sees it and can change it in Profile. There is no "Is this your timezone?" screen: this differs from story 2 in `.scratch/pact-v0/spec.md` (ticket 22). Migration 30 gave UTC to accounts made before this that never confirmed one. Test: `sign-in.test.ts`.

## Loading screens

- A tab or page opened again shows what it last loaded at once while it loads afresh; if that load fails, it keeps showing it with "couldn't load" above. When the app opens it loads the tabs not shown, so the first visit to each is quick too. Forms and pickers (editing or changing a pact, planning a vacation, the deck and reviewer pickers, notification settings) always load afresh (`useLoad`'s `key`, `src/client/ui.tsx`; `src/client/last-loaded.ts`). A pact's page is remembered like a tab: its name, reviewer and reminders changes send only their own term. Test: `last-loaded.test.tsx`; `PactPage.test.tsx`, "a pact page opened again shows the pact as last loaded".
- Any command the person sends, refused or not, and signing out forget everything loaded, so no screen shows what was there before their own action; the app then loads the four tabs again in the background, so they are quick again about a round trip later. Signing out stops those loads first. The app's own checks, push subscriptions and previews forget nothing (`post` and `postQuietly`, `src/client/api.ts`; `reloadWhenForgotten`, `src/client/last-loaded.ts`). Test: `api.test.ts`, `last-loaded.test.tsx`.

## Pull to refresh

- Each of the four tabs (Today, Pacts, Review, Ledgers) has pull to refresh; pages opened from a tab can't be pulled, though the bar moves on them too when they refresh by themselves (see Coming back to the front). A pull starts only with one finger at the top of the tab; a finger that first moves up is a scroll. While it pulls, the content follows at half the finger's travel, up to 120 px, and a line under the header grows to full width at 64 px. Letting go past that springs the content back and runs the tab's refresh, with a moving bar under the header until it settles, whether it loaded or failed, or until the tab is left, so the next tab can be pulled; letting go short of it, or a cancelled touch, does nothing. A pull while a refresh runs, including one the tab runs by itself, is ignored: the bar keeps moving for the one running, and no second one starts. The bar moves whatever the Reduce Motion setting says. Touch only: there is no refresh button (`PullToRefresh`, `useRefresh`, `src/client/pull-to-refresh.tsx`; `.scratch/pull-to-refresh/spec.md`). Test: `pull-to-refresh.test.tsx`; `coming-back.test.tsx`, "a pull while it runs".
- Today first checks Oura now and AnkiWeb now together, whatever cards it shows (the core asks only about nights and days still waiting), skipping a service whose connection needs reconnecting, then reloads. A pull while Today's own check of a service runs waits for that check instead of sending another, and shows what that check found. Each card shows its check as when Today checks by itself ("Checking Oura…", "Checking AnkiWeb…"). When a check a pull sent or joined answers that the service couldn't be reached, the No Oura data card or the Anki card says "Couldn't reach Oura. Pact will try again." or "Couldn't reach AnkiWeb. Pact will try again." until a later check of that service answers ok, while Today stays open; Today's own checks, and those of a refresh Today runs by itself, never show it unless a pull is waiting for one (`useCheckNow`, `src/client/Today.tsx`). Test: `Today.test.tsx`, "a pull on Today".
- Pacts and Ledgers reload their list; Review reloads its queue and the list waiting on a reply. A reload keeps what was shown until it answers, and a failed one shows "couldn't load" above it, as any reload does (`useLoad`, `src/client/ui.tsx`). A pull forgets nothing else loaded.
- While any sheet is open, the page behind it doesn't scroll and can't be pulled; it keeps its place for when the last sheet closes. iOS WebKit can send a drag on a sheet to the scroll area under it (bugs.webkit.org 117059), scrolling the page and leaving the sheet stuck, which it can't do while that area's overflow is hidden. A scroll that reaches a sheet's top or bottom stays in the sheet (`holdPageStill`, `src/client/ui.tsx`; `.sheet-open`, `src/client/styles.css`). Test: `sheet.test.tsx`.
- Any sheet can be dragged down to close it, from anywhere on it, but only with one finger while it is scrolled to its top; a finger that first moves up or sideways, or one in the text field being typed in, scrolls or edits as usual. The sheet moves once the finger has gone 6 px down, then follows it; dragged up, it moves at most 12 px. Letting go more than a third of its height down, or moving down faster than 0.5 px per ms over the last 100 ms or so, closes it; otherwise, or on a cancelled touch, it springs back. However the sheet itself closes (a drag, Done or Cancel, a tap on the dimmed area, Escape) it slides down while the dimming fades, for 0.2 s, and only then closes, once however often it is asked; under Reduce Motion it closes at once, though a drag still moves it. The page behind stays still until it is gone. A sheet its screen takes away, after a send that went through or from a button inside it, goes at once. Closing throws away anything typed, with no confirmation, and is allowed while a send runs, which carries on. Touch only: a mouse can't drag a sheet (`useSheetMotion`, `src/client/sheet-motion.ts`; `Sheet`, `src/client/ui.tsx`; `.scratch/sheet-drag-to-close/spec.md`). Test: `sheet.test.tsx`.

## Coming back to the front

- iOS often keeps Pact in memory while other apps are used, and shows it as it was on coming back. So when the app comes back to the front (`visibilitychange` to visible), the shown screen refreshes by itself if it last loaded more than 10 minutes ago, or if the day in the profile's timezone differs from the day it last loaded. Opening the screen counts as a load, and so does a refresh, a pull or one by itself, that loaded, from when that try started; one that failed doesn't count, so the next return refreshes again however soon. A short trip away does nothing (`RELOAD_AFTER_MS`, `useReloadWhenDue`, `src/client/coming-back.ts`; `PullToRefresh`, `src/client/pull-to-refresh.tsx`). Test: `coming-back.test.tsx`, "when the shown screen refreshes by itself".
- While the app is in front, the shown screen refreshes by itself at midnight in the profile's timezone. A timer waits for midnight, working it out again at least every hour and on each return, so a daylight saving change, or iOS freezing the app, can't make it late (`useReloadWhenDue`). Today's header shows the day the cards Today loaded are for, as the core says (`date`, `todayPage`, `src/core/today.ts`), so it turns to the new day with the cards, on coming back and at midnight alike; while Today's reload is out, or if it fails, the header keeps the old day above the old cards. Until Today first loads, and if that first load fails, the header shows the day by the clock in the profile's timezone (`onDate`, `Today.tsx`; `Shell`, `src/client/App.tsx`). Test: `coming-back.test.tsx`, "Today's header turns to the new day with its cards at midnight…", "Today's header stays on the old day…", "Today's header shows the new day after a short trip away across midnight"; `App.test.tsx`, "until Today first loads…"; `today.test.ts`, "says the owner's date…".
- What refreshes is what a pull runs on the shown tab (on Today, checking Oura and AnkiWeb, then reloading Today). On a page that shows what it loads (a pact, a ledger, an excuse, evidence, a payment, a change request) that page reloads. A form (New pact, Edit pact, Request change, Vacation, Notifications) registers no refresh, so it never reloads by itself (`useRefresh`, `src/client/pull-to-refresh.tsx`). Test: `coming-back.test.tsx`, "in the app".
- The Review tab's badge, shown on every page, reloads by itself by the same rule, from when it last loaded: on coming back after more than 10 minutes or on a new day, and at midnight, forms included. On the Review tab the tab's own refresh loads the queue and sets the badge, so the queue loads once (`useReviewCount`, `App.tsx`). Test: `coming-back.test.tsx`, "a form page never reloads by itself, though the Review badge does", "on Review, back after 11 minutes…".
- The bar under the header moves while it runs, on a tab or a page alike. One refresh runs at a time: a pull while it runs is ignored and it keeps the bar. A refresh by itself that comes due while another runs (midnight during a pull, say) runs once that one settles, if the screen is still due then (`PullToRefresh`). Test: `coming-back.test.tsx`, "the bar moves while it runs…", "a pull that answers after midnight is followed by midnight's refresh".
- It is quiet at first: right after a phone wakes its network is often not ready. If it fails, the screen stays as it was, with no "couldn't load", and it tries once more after 3 seconds, or as soon as the browser says it is online, whichever comes first; the bar keeps moving meanwhile. Only if that second try fails does the screen show its usual "couldn't load" line (`RETRY_AFTER_MS`, `PullToRefresh`; `useLoad`'s `reload({ quiet })`, `src/client/ui.tsx`). The checks of Oura and AnkiWeb in either try never show "Couldn't reach Oura/AnkiWeb"; only a pull's do. Test: `coming-back.test.tsx`, "a refresh by itself that fails"; `Today.test.tsx`, "coming back to the front".
- While any sheet is open, it waits, and runs when the last sheet closes and is gone, if the screen is still due then; the badge waits the same way. A change to the profile's timezone made meanwhile doesn't drop it (`anySheetOpen`, `onLastSheetClosed`, `src/client/ui.tsx`). Test: `coming-back.test.tsx`, "while a sheet is open…", "waiting for a sheet…".
- Today still checks Oura or AnkiWeb on every return while a No Oura data card or an Anki To do card shows, however short the trip, then reloads. On a return that refreshes Today, it doesn't: the refresh checks each service once and loads Today once. With a sheet open, nothing is checked until the sheet closes; then the refresh checks (`onComingBack`, `coming-back.ts`; `useCheckNow`, `Today.tsx`). Test: `Today.test.tsx`, "coming back to the front".
